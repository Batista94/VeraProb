# Test Plan: 20260821000002_fix_acknowledge_portal_status.sql

## Purpose
Ensures that a carrier can accept a sanction ("De Acordo") when the queue status is `disputed`, effectively allowing them to surrender without uploading counter-evidence.

## Scenarios
1. **Applied Status (Existing Behavior)**
   - Condition: Queue entry status is `applied`.
   - Expected Output: Acknowledgement is processed, status becomes `acknowledged`, ledger fact recorded.

2. **Disputed Status (New Behavior)**
   - Condition: Queue entry status is `disputed`.
   - Expected Output: Acknowledgement is processed successfully, status becomes `acknowledged`, ledger fact recorded.

3. **Other Statuses (E.g. pending)**
   - Condition: Queue entry status is not `applied` or `disputed`.
   - Expected Output: Must raise `insufficient_privilege` (42501).
